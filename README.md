About
==========

[Shard Shulker Boxes](https://modrinth.com/mod/shard-shulker) is a Minecraft mod that introduces three new types of craftable Shulker Boxes. In order to upgrade your Shulker Boxes, you will need to head underground to mine some amethyst, then find an ocean monument and defeat its Guardians and finally, search the ancient city for echo shards.

This mod was made as an alternative to Iron-styled storage mods and it gives more purpose to amethyst, prismarine and echo shards. The amethyst shulker box has the capacity of a double chest, the prismarine one, three chests and the echo one, four chests.

### Mods for a balanced experience and better progression :
- [Bag of Holding](https://modrinth.com/mod/bag-of-holding) : Early/Mid-Game backpack mod balanced compared to other backpack mods so as to not make shulker boxes useless.
- [End Remastered](https://modrinth.com/mod/endrem) : Getting to the End requires more exploration which truly turns Shulker Boxes into End-Game backpacks.
